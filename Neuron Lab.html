Neuron Lab

Three small experiments for Neural Networks (BC05001061): build a neuron, train a perceptron, and watch gradient descent. Predict first, then change a control and check.

1. A neuron with two inputs

Curve: activation f(z). Dot: your neuron's current z and output.

Hint: z = w1·x1 + w2·x2 + b. Increase the bias and watch the dot slide right along the curve. Which activations can output a negative value?

2. Perceptron trainer

Filled circle = class 1. Hollow square = class 0. Line = decision boundary. Axes run from -0.5 to 1.5.

Hint: the rule is w ← w + η(target − output)·x. Train AND and OR until errors reach 0. Then try XOR: no single line separates the classes, which is why hidden layers exist.

3. Gradient descent

Loss: L(w) = (w − 3)² + 1. Minimum at w = 3.

Line: loss after each iteration (0 = start). Dashed line: minimum loss 1.

Hint: the update is w ← w − η·2(w − 3). Try η = 0.01 (slow), 0.1 (smooth), 0.9 (zig-zags), 1.1 (loss grows). That is why learning rate matters.
